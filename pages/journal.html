Enter the Conversation

Ideas aren’t meant to replace thought. They’re meant to provoke it.

The Synchronic Self Theory Journal is where philosophy meets practice. It’s a place to examine the patterns shaping daily experience, learn how symbolic systems work, and explore perspectives without being told what conclusion you’re required to reach.

The journal is organized into three distinct areas. The Theory in Day-to-Day Life focuses on reflection and philosophical inquiry. Tarot and Runes focus more directly on education, technique, tools, and informed practice.

Each section has a different purpose, but all three share the same foundation: symbols become most useful when they help us ask clearer questions, recognize our own projections, and think more deliberately about the lives we’re creating.

What to Expect

Three topics, each serving a different purpose.

The Theory in Day-to-Day Life

This section uses Synchronic Self Theory as a lens rather than a rulebook. Its purpose is to leave readers with new perspectives to consider for themselves. The writing may examine perception, projection, relationships, identity, symbolism, choice, and the patterns that become visible when inner and outer experience begin reflecting one another.

Tarot

This section is built around useful, educational content. Expect practical how-to guides, explanations of reading methods, discussions of card symbolism, deck reviews, and observations drawn from working with different tarot systems. It’s meant to help readers understand the tools, not merely admire them from across a velvet table.

Runes

This section focuses on learning how to work with runes thoughtfully. It includes casting methods, interpretation techniques, rune-set reviews, practical considerations, and educational material designed to make the system more approachable without stripping away its depth.

Read, Reflect, Decide

A mirror can offer perspective. It can’t do your thinking for you.

The journal isn’t built around fixed answers. It’s built around better questions, practical knowledge, and the freedom to decide what meaning you carry forward.
